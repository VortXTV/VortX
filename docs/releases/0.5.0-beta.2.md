# 0.5.0 Beta 2 — Profile Recovery Hotfix

**Install this over Beta 1.** This hotfix repairs a problem that could stop the profile screen from opening a viewer or saving a new profile. It also replaces the plain profile list with a cinematic profile picker. Apple build **261** and Android version code **261** share this release number. Both Android distributions and all four Apple packages are part of this release; the profile recovery and picker changes described below are Apple changes.

Your existing profiles, add-ons, Library, Watchlist and watch history are kept. You do not need to delete the app, reset your account or create replacement profiles to install this update.

## What's fixed

**The profile screen waits until profiles are ready before opening or saving one.** Beta 1 could show your saved profiles before the app had finished preparing their account. Tapping a profile or saving a new one then used an unavailable account connection and failed immediately. Opening, creating, editing and removing a profile now wait for the current account's preparation, then use its fresh result. A response from an older account or profile cannot complete a newer action.

**A saved profile is opened only after the change succeeds.** The app no longer treats the visible list as proof that its account is ready. Failed actions keep you on the screen, preserve your edits and offer Try Again or Account Settings. A failed new-profile save does not clear the form.

**Signing in has a clear route from the profile screen.** If VortX is signed out, the screen offers Sign In instead of leaving you with profile buttons that cannot work. Your optional Stremio connection is separate from your VortX account. The app does not ask you to reconnect Stremio as a way to open your VortX profiles.

**Editing a locked profile no longer asks for its PIN twice or closes the editor after unlocking.** Unlocking the selected profile keeps its editor open. Opening a profile normally still completes the profile picker only after the switch succeeds.

**Profile errors use normal language.** The large technical paragraphs about older settings, account bindings and the native engine are removed from the picker. Errors explain what you can do next, without displaying account identifiers or private details. Diagnostics record the failed preparation stage to help investigate a remaining failure.

## What's new

**A cinematic “Who's watching?” screen on Apple.** A movie background fades behind a responsive grid of rounded-square profile avatars. Each viewer keeps their name, chosen accent, current-profile marker and PIN indicator. Add Profile and Edit are part of the same grid; editing offers Done.

The background comes from public movie artwork, not your private viewing history. It is optional: profiles remain usable when artwork cannot load. Phone, tablet, Mac and Apple TV layouts adapt to the available space and larger text. Apple TV keeps a visible focus ring, and PIN forms can scroll on small screens without hiding Unlock or Cancel.

## Retained from Beta 1

**This is an update to the complete 0.5 app, not a stripped-down profile build.** It keeps the native account, catalog and streaming paths, encrypted account state, profile-owned add-on visibility and order, durable Watchlist changes, history-preserving migration and the website's acknowledged add-on edit path.

**Cinema and discovery remain.** Home and Browse, Quick View, larger cards, catalog See All, Search, Library, Watchlist, History, Downloads, episode details and add-on source tabs remain. Mac keeps horizontal navigation and its native window controls. Android keeps both phone and TV interfaces, the Cinema navigation structure and remote-friendly source navigation.

**The earlier playback repairs remain.** Exact Continue Watching episode targeting, owned next-episode preparation, chosen-release/audio-language continuity, pause preservation during accepted source/player changes, pending-seek ownership and failed-episode Retry remain. The Dolby Vision attachment-timer repair, HLS startup readiness repair, typed mpv cache-state handling, complete request headers and source-specific User-Agent isolation remain.

**The earlier player and Usenet features remain.** Subtitle selection, timing and background styles; seek-bar styles; aspect controls; audio tracks; chapters; preview return; external-player handoff; supported Picture-in-Picture; and configurable intro/credits skipping with Off and Cancel are retained. Native Usenet keeps bounded concurrent read-ahead, saved-provider alternatives, NZB/indexer sources, explicit file/episode selection and cancellation tied to the playback or download that owns it. Full and Lite retain their different embedded-server capabilities.

**Integrations and downloads remain.** Local/Trakt/SIMKL Continue Watching choices, Trakt/SIMKL settings, optional Stremio import, live TV, collections, pairing, diagnostics, offline downloads, watched-download controls, Newznab indexers and ordered NNTP servers are kept. Android Full remains the mpv distribution with Media3 fallback; Android Play remains the Media3 distribution without GPL player libraries.

For the complete preceding change list, see the [Beta 1 release notes](https://github.com/VortXTV/VortX/releases/tag/v0.5.0-beta.1).

## Verification and remaining limits

The Apple profile changes have independent source and interface reviews, profile-preparation regression tests and iOS, tvOS and macOS SDK type checks. The existing account, stale-response and persisted-state checks are retained. Release packages must also pass the separate native-content, version, signing, launch, checksum and update-feed gates before publication.

**The reported iPhone must still confirm the result.** Beta 1's log did not record why the first account preparation was unavailable. This hotfix repairs the proven premature-action failure and records preparation failures more clearly; it does not prove that every possible sign-in, network or account-restore failure is gone.

**This is not a claim that every playback or sync issue is fixed.** Sustained Dolby Vision playback, AIOStreams/nonzero-offset freezes, NNTP throughput, frame pacing and physical cross-device sync still need current device and provider checks. Compressed/encrypted Usenet archives and PAR2 repair remain unsupported. Android is included, but this hotfix does not claim complete Apple/Android visual or feature parity.

Unfinished Android batch-download/cache changes and other unverified work are not added merely to enlarge this hotfix. They remain separate work rather than being presented as tested release fixes.

## Please test

- On the iPhone or iPad that failed in Beta 1, open your existing profile, then create and save a new profile. Try editing a locked profile and returning to the picker.
- Restart the app and switch between your owner and other profiles. Check that each viewer keeps their add-ons, history and Watchlist.
- If VortX is signed out, use the new Sign In action. A Stremio-only connection is not a VortX account sign-in.
- Try the picker with larger text and on Apple TV with the remote. If an action still fails, export fresh diagnostics without deleting the app or resetting your account.
- Recheck playback, Continue Watching and next-episode behavior. Include the build, device, title, source and preceding action with any new report.
